About Acctentity Consultancy

Your partner in accounting, financial management and business growth

We are a professional accounting and business advisory firm committed to helping businesses keep their finances accurate, meet compliance requirements and make informed decisions.

Through accounting, taxation, audit, financial advisory, company registration and ERP solutions, we give businesses the support they need to operate with confidence.

Who we are

Professional financial solutions built around your business

Acctentity Consultancy is a financial and business advisory firm located at Greenhouse, Ngong Road, Nairobi, Kenya.

Managing a business means complex financial responsibilities, from keeping accounting records and meeting tax obligations to handling corporate documents and setting up effective financial systems. We bring together professional accounting services and technology-driven solutions to meet those needs.

We work with entrepreneurs, small and medium-sized enterprises, established companies and organisations that need reliable financial and business support.

Our focus goes beyond handling records. We help clients understand their financial position, improve their systems and make decisions that support long-term success.

Our mission

To provide reliable, professional and practical accounting, taxation, audit and financial advisory solutions that help businesses maintain compliance, improve financial management and achieve sustainable growth.

Our vision

To become a trusted accounting and financial solutions partner, recognised for professional excellence, innovation and commitment to helping businesses succeed.
